THE CREATIVE MIND BEHIND THE WORK

Design-minded.
People-focused.

I’m an Interior Architecture and Design student with a passion for creating spaces that are thoughtful, functional, and visually engaging.

My approach brings creativity together with organization, collaboration, and problem-solving. I enjoy moving from research and early concepts to plans, material selections, and visualizations that help communicate a design clearly.

I’m always looking for opportunities to learn from experienced designers, contribute to a team, and grow through real-world projects. I value flexibility, attention to detail, and the idea that good design should support the people who use a space.
